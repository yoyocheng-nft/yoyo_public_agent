Executive Summary

Generated by LLM: minimax/minimax-m2.5

In Nov 2026, BofA total card spending held firm at +5.2% YoY after softening from September’s +6.2%, with online retail (+6.1%) outpacing brick-and-mortar (+2.8%). Strength was concentrated in gas (+20.1%), airlines (+8.7%), and general merchandise (+6.9%), while furniture (−6.9%) and department stores (−4.6%) remained the main soft spots.

Key Findings

  • Gas led categories at +20.1% YoY; airlines followed at +8.7%, while lodging lagged at only +0.5%.
