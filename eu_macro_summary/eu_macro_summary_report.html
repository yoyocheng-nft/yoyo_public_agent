Definitions

Sentix Investor Confidence

Monthly Sentix survey of private and institutional investors on the euro-area economy (SNTEEUGX). The headline is a balance of optimists minus pessimists, from −100 to +100, with zero as neutral. It blends the current situation and the six-month outlook. Bloomberg’s long name has no SA/NSA label, so the row is untagged.

Harmonized CPI vs CPI

Harmonized CPI is Eurostat’s HICP. The basket rules are the same across countries, owner-occupied housing is excluded, and the YoY rates in these tables are not seasonally adjusted. Eurozone headline and core (ECCPEMUY, CPEXEMUY) and Germany headline (GRCP2HYY) are HICP.

National CPI is a country’s own index. Germany’s Destatis CPI uses a different basket, including some owner-occupied housing that HICP leaves out, so the two YoY rates differ.

Germany retail sales

Eurostat retail sales volume (RSWADEYY) is working-day adjusted and excludes motor vehicles and motorcycles. Vehicle sales are a separate trade series, not part of this retail print.
