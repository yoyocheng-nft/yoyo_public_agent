Definitions

S&P Global UK PMI

Diffusion-index PMIs compiled by S&P Global for the UK (formerly Markit). Readings above 50 indicate expansion vs the prior month.

  • Manufacturing / Services / Composite — tickers MPMIGBMA, MPMIGBSA, MPMIGBCA.
  • Components — new orders, output, employment, and supplier delivery times drive the headline; services dominates UK GDP weight.
  • Use — early read on activity breadth; watch divergence between manufacturing (trade-sensitive) and services (domestic demand).

GfK Consumer Confidence

Monthly household survey (UKCCI) measuring sentiment toward personal finances and the general economic situation. Not seasonally adjusted in Bloomberg; higher = more optimistic.

  • Leading signal — often moves ahead of retail spending and housing appetite.
  • Context — sensitive to inflation prints, mortgage rates, and fiscal headlines.

UK Gilt 10Y Yield & BoE

The 10-year UK gilt yield (GUKG10) is the benchmark long nominal rate for sovereign funding and discounting.

  • Lower yield — typically easier financial conditions, weaker growth/inflation expectations, or safe-haven demand.
  • Higher yield — firmer activity/inflation expectations, term premium, or reduced duration demand.
  • BoE — Bank Rate is the policy anchor; gilt yields reflect the expected path of rates plus term premium. The RHS chart pairs 10Y yields with USD/GBP spot.

CPI vs RPI

Both measure UK inflation YoY, but the baskets and formulae differ.

  • CPI / Core CPI (UKRPCJYR, UKHCA9IQ) — official BoE target measure; excludes owner-occupier housing costs in headline CPI; core strips energy/food/alcohol/tobacco.
  • RPI (UKRPYOY) — older index with arithmetic mean and housing components; tends to run higher; still used for some index-linked gilts and regulated prices.
  • Dashboard coloring — inflation rows are inverted (green = lower inflation).
