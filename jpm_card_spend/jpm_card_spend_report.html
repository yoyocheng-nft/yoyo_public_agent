Executive Summary

Generated by LLM: minimax/minimax-m2.5

November 2026 JPM card spend remained solidly expansionary, with total spending up 6.9% YoY and nondiscretionary categories outpacing discretionary (+9.1% vs +5.6%). In-person activity was the clear driver—card-present spend surged 11.3% while card-not-present lagged at +1.4%. Strength concentrated in health care, wholesale/discount retail, restaurants, and especially recurring nondiscretionary payments (+26.6%), while gas stations were the only major drag (-3.4%).

Key Findings

Generated by LLM: minimax/minimax-m2.5

  • Total spending rose +6.9% YoY (+6.0% ex-gas), with nondiscretionary (+9.1%) clearly outpacing discretionary (+5.6%).
  • Card-present spend jumped +11.3% vs only +1.4% for card-not-present, pointing to a strong brick-and-mortar rebound.
  • Recurring nondiscretionary payments surged +26.6%, the fastest category gain; recurring discretionary also rose +9.6%.
  • Sector leaders included Wholesale Clubs & Discount Stores (+10.2%), Health Care & Pharmacies (+10.0%), and Restaurants (+8.6%); Airlines (+7.1%) also firmed.
  • Gas Stations fell -3.4% YoY—the lone notable decline—while Travel & Entertainment (+3.5%) and Lodging (+3.7%) grew more moderately.
